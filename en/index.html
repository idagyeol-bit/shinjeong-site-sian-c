In hazardous spaces, robots go in firstFive steps on site — from preparing the robot to handling the waste
  1. 01

    Unmanned robots we developed ourselves

    We developed generations 1–5 of our unmanned vacuum robot in-house and set up a corporate R&D center in 2017. The technology is protected by registered patents.

  2. 02

    We send in the right robot for your equipment

    We choose a robot to suit the tank, pond or reactor and send it in through the manhole. Its hydraulic drive lets it work in explosion-proof areas.

  3. 03

    The robot goes in first to recover material

    The robot enters the equipment first and recovers sludge and hazardous residue. Operators watch on CCTV from a control vehicle outside and steer it remotely.

  4. 04

    Workers finish once the hazard is lower

    The robot's front jet nozzle washes the interior to reduce the hazard. Then workers go in and remove the final residue.

  5. 05

    Recovered material is reduced and handled

    We separate solids from liquid with a screw decanter and filter press to reduce the volume, and also collect and transport general and designated waste.
